Who can use Smartstaff plus?

Super Admin manages the platform. Company administrators and branch managers use the web console; employees use the Android app for fieldwork. Each account sees the tools allowed by its role and company policy.

How does face attendance work?

Staff register three reference photos and save the registration on the server. When enabled, a live selfie is compared with that registration before GPS check-in. Office attendance may also require being near a configured location.

Can I integrate my own application?

Yes. Submit the developer onboarding form. The Super Admin reviews the request, binds access to a company and chooses read scopes, expiry and request limits. See the developer guide for the current API resources.

Where do I get a login?

Your company administrator issues your employee ID, mobile login or email and password. Public website visitors cannot create staff accounts. Contact your organization for access.
